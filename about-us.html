About Manar Al Omran

Five decades of pioneering formwork engineering, industrial scaffolding solutions, and structural integrity across the Middle East and Africa.

Company Overview

50 Years of Engineering & Industrial Leadership

Founded in 1974 under the name of Salem Bin Hindi Al Harbi Establishment, Manar Al Omran Trading & Industrial Co. Ltd. (MAO) has developed into one of the largest and most trusted providers of formwork and scaffolding solutions across Saudi Arabia, the GCC, and Africa.

Our core business encompasses three integrated pillars: industrial scaffolding services, structural concrete casting services (including full on-site installation, inspection, and dismantling), and the sale and rental of heavy-duty scaffolding and formwork materials.

With an active rental inventory exceeding 150,000 tons, advanced manufacturing facilities, and certified engineering centers, we provide end-to-end temporary works support to regional infrastructure mega-projects, petrochemical complexes, and high-rise commercial developments.

✓
Multi-National Presence: Operating major branches and staging depots across 6+ countries.
✓
Turnkey Field Execution: Certified installation, dismantling, and on-site load monitoring teams.
✓
Massive Fleet Agility: Immediate delivery of Ringlock, Cuplock, Profex, and Shorbrace systems.

HERITAGE TIMELINE // 1974 – PRESENT

FIVE DECADES OF INDUSTRIAL GROWTH

From a pioneering Saudi establishment in 1974 to a multinational formwork and scaffolding conglomerate across the Middle East and Africa.

1974

Founding in Dammam

Established as Salem Bin Hindi Al Harbi Establishment in Dammam, Eastern Province, introducing standardized scaffolding and shuttering materials to the Kingdom's rapidly expanding construction sector.

1995

Manufacturing & GCC Expansion

Transitioned to Manar Al Omran Trading & Industrial Co. Ltd., establishing automated steel fabrication plants and opening strategic regional headquarters in Riyadh, Jeddah, UAE, and Bahrain.

2010

African Footprint & Mega Hire Fleet

Expanded operations into Egypt (Cairo, Alexandria, 6th of October), South Africa (Johannesburg), and Kenya, establishing massive rental depots and certified temporary works engineering divisions.

2026+

3D BIM & Vision 2030 Projects

Pioneering integrated 3D BIM clash detection, hydraulic auto-climbing systems, and specialized civil engineering falsework for Saudi Vision 2030 landmark mega-developments and energy projects.
