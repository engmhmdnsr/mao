Cuplock System

Proven node-point locking system securing up to 4 horizontal components securely in a single action.

4-in-1 Node Locking
System Scaffolding

System Overview & Engineering Concept

The Cuplock Scaffolding System is a time-tested, heavy-duty modular scaffolding solution recognized across the Middle East for its rapid assembly, structural strength, and zero loose fittings.

Featuring fixed lower cups and ductile cast top cups spaced at 500 mm intervals on high-tensile steel standards, Cuplock locks up to four horizontal ledgers securely in a single hammer rotation.

Cuplock effortlessly transitions between exterior building facade access and heavy-duty slab propping falsework, delivering exceptional return on investment across commercial and civil sites.

Leg Load Capacity Up to 57 kN per Leg
Cup Node Spacing 500 mm Fixed Intervals
Tube Specification 48.3 mm OD x 3.2 mm / 3.8 mm
Standard Compliance BS 1139 Part 5 / EN 12810

TECHNICAL ADVANTAGES

KEY ENGINEERING FEATURES

Designed and manufactured for high performance, load safety, and maximum site productivity.

4-in-1 Node Locking

Upper cup rotates and locks down over four forged ledger ends in one action.

Zero Loose Fittings

Captive locking mechanism eliminates lost clamps, loose nuts, and site delays.

Dual Access & Falsework

Seamlessly converts from facade scaffolding into heavy load-bearing slab shoring.

Heavy Forged Durability

Forged node points resist aggressive site handling and heavy cyclic usage.

High Torsional Rigidity

Rigid node connections provide exceptional column buckling resistance.

Complete Component Range

Hop-up brackets, cantilever beam brackets, and internal stairs integrate easily.
