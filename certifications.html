REGULATORY APPROVALS

STRICT COMPLIANCE WITH GLOBAL & REGIONAL CODES

Our structural engineering designs and manufactured hardware are tested and pre-qualified for the most demanding civil and industrial specifications.

Saudi Aramco & SABIC Standards

Pre-qualified scaffolding materials and certified erection procedures conforming to Saudi Aramco Construction Safety Manual (CSM) and SABIC industrial safety directives.

BS EN 12810 & BS EN 12811 (Facade & Access)

All Ringlock and Cuplock modular components satisfy European standards for temporary works equipment, load class ratings, and deflection limits.

DIN 18218 Fresh Concrete Pressure

Profex and Profemax heavy wall systems are engineered to withstand fresh concrete lateral pressures up to 80 kN/m² with certified safety factors.

ASTM Steel Grades & Hot-Dip Galvanizing

Manufactured using certified Q235 and Q345 high-yield alloy steels, hot-dip galvanized to ASTM A123 / ISO 1461 for maximum corrosion resistance in coastal environments.

SASO & GCC Civil Defense Approvals

All temporary access towers, public egress stairs, and heavy bridge shoring systems are vetted and approved by SASO and municipal authorities across Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Bahrain.

Independent Destructive Load Testing

Periodic batch testing conducted at accredited regional testing laboratories for weld penetration, tensile strength, and buckling load capacities.
