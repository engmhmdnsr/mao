THE 4 PHASES OF STRUCTURAL CONCRETE FORMING

How Manar Al Omran engineers support every structural element from deep foundations to high-rise slabs.

PHASE 01 // FOUNDATIONS & WALLS
STAGE 01

Heavy Wall & Elevator Core Formwork

Modular heavy-duty steel framed panels engineered to resist hydrostatic concrete pressure up to 80 kN/m² with high-speed crane gang cycling. Eliminates form deflection and ensures pristine fair-faced concrete finish.

Primary System Profemax & Profex
Pressure Resistance 80 kN / m² (DIN 18218)
Crane Handling Gangable Large Units
Applications Basements, Cores, Piers
